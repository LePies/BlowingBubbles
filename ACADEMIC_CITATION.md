# Academic citation (published scientific work)

The **software** is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You may use,
modify, and redistribute it without asking for permission.

## When to cite

Please cite BlowingBubbles **only if** you describe or rely on this software in
**published scientific work**, for example:

- peer-reviewed journal or conference papers
- theses and dissertations made publicly available
- preprints on arXiv or similar servers
- citable technical reports in institutional or public repositories

Citation is **not** required for private study, internal prototyping, teaching
demos, or unpublished exploratory analysis.

## How to cite

Copy the BibTeX entry from [`CITATION.bib`](CITATION.bib) (or use
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) with citation-file-format tools). Update the
`TODO` fields when the accompanying paper is published.
